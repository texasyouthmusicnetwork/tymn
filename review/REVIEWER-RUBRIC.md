# Reviewer rubric — TYMN Fall Kickoff Challenge

Keep this open while reviewing. Two lines to hold in your head:

- **Valid = a genuine student performance, honestly represented.** Not "good."
- **Standout = you would feature it** on TYMN's site or socials.

TYMN rewards participation and effort over raw talent. A beginner playing
"Twinkle Twinkle" slowly with wrong notes is **Valid**. You are only screening
out non-performances, misrepresentation, and point-farming.

---

## `Link OK?` (mostly auto-filled)

| Value | When |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | Video plays and it's the submitted performance |
| **No — private** | 401/embedding-off, or asks for access → link re-request (Step 6) |
| **No — dead / wrong link** | 404, removed, or points at something unrelated |
| **Can't tell** | Rare — loads but you genuinely can't judge |

If it's not "Yes", stop there — don't set `Valid?` yet.

## `Valid?` — the participation gate

**Yes** — all of these hold:
1. A real musical performance by the submitter (or their named ensemble).
2. Roughly a complete piece, movement, etude, or a substantial section —
   as a floor, **~1 minute of actual playing**. Not a 15-second clip.
3. It's a live performance of the person on screen — not a pro recording,
   not someone else's YouTube video, not a lip-sync over a track.
4. You can see and hear the performer actually playing/singing.

**No:**
- Not music / joke / troll submission (the two `dQw4w9WgXcQ` test rows).
- Clearly not the submitter performing.
- Too short or incomplete to be a performance (a few seconds, tuning, noodling).
- **Duplicate** — the same student's same piece/video already submitted. Keep the
  first, mark later ones `No`, write "duplicate" in Notes.
- Misrepresented — AI-generated, or edited to fake the performance.

**Needs 2nd look:**
- Borderline length or effort.
- Not sure it's the submitter.
- Possible duplicate, not certain.
- Category genuinely ambiguous *and* it affects scoring.

> When unsure, use **Needs 2nd look** — never guess `Yes`/`No`.

### Point-farming (each valid submission is 100 RP)

- One student, several **different** pieces → all valid. Encouraged.
- One student, **same** piece repeated → first valid, rest `No — duplicate`.
- One ensemble video submitted by **each member separately** → counts **once**.
  Mark the first `Valid`, the rest `No` ("ensemble already counted, row N").
- A burst of throwaway clips from one school (tuning notes, 10-sec fragments) →
  each `No — not a performance`, and flag it to an organizer in Notes.

## `School (canonical)` (mostly auto-filled)

Pick the leaderboard name from the dropdown. Not listed → **TYMN Review → Add
selected row's school to Schools tab**. Leave blank for out-of-state / "not
competing for a school" submissions — that's valid, just no school points.

## `Standout?` — Pass 1 nomination (check the box)

Nominate if the performance stands out **for any level** — polish, musicality,
expression, a hard passage handled well, or a beginner whose serious work clearly
shows. Test: *would I want this on TYMN's page?*

- **Nominate generously** — Pass 2 filters. Over-nominating is fine.
- Don't nominate on piece difficulty or camera/audio quality alone.

## `Standout confirmed?` — Pass 2 (a *different* reviewer than Pass 1)

Watch the whole video. **Yes** if it holds up as featured-quality — musical,
committed, clean *for the performer's evident level*, enjoyable to watch. **No**
if it's solid but not exceptional.

- When calls are close, spread confirmations across **categories and schools** so
  features aren't all one instrument from one program.
- **Check the consent column** (`Opt-out/opt-in`). If the student marked
  *"I DO NOT consent to my video being featured"* → see decision #2 below before
  confirming.

Confirmed standouts become **Outstanding Performers** (feature + 400 RP).

## Category boundaries

| Category | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Solo Keyboard** | Piano, digital piano, organ, harpsichord, celesta |
| **Solo Strings** | Violin, viola, cello, bass, harp, **guitar** (any), ukulele, mandolin, banjo — bowed or plucked |
| **Solo Wind** | All woodwinds (flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon, sax, recorder) and all brass (trumpet, horn, trombone, euphonium, tuba) |
| **Solo Percussion** | Snare, marimba, xylophone, vibraphone, timpani, drum set, hand/mallet percussion |
| **Solo Misc.** | Voice, non-Western instruments (erhu, guzheng, sitar, tabla…), synth/electronic solo, anything else |
| **Ensemble** | 2+ performers with independent parts — duet, trio, quartet, band |

- **Wrong category → fix it, don't reject.** Category feeds the school's
  instrument-variety bonus, so accuracy matters.
- **Soloist + piano accompanist** = judged as a **solo** in the soloist's
  category. Accompaniment doesn't make it an ensemble.
- Multiple instruments in one solo video → pick the primary, or Solo Misc.; note it.

## Decisions needed from organizers (set these before reviewing starts)

1. **Ensemble counting.** Rubric currently says one ensemble video = **one**
   valid submission for the school, regardless of headcount. Keep, or count
   per named performer? (Per-performer is a Step 5 scoring change, not a
   reviewer call.)
2. **Non-consenting standouts.** If a student opted out of being featured, can
   they still be an Outstanding Performer for the **400 RP** (just not featured),
   or are they ineligible for the designation entirely?
3. **Minimum length.** ~1 minute of playing is the draft floor for `Valid`.
   Raise, lower, or keep?
4. **Late edits.** A student who fixes a broken link after Sept 30 — still
   counts, or not?
