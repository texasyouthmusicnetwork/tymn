Our Mission

There's more to music than what's on the sheet.

Texas Youth Music Network was founded to solve a recurring problem in music education: a lot of talented and ambitious student musicians don't recieve the opportunities they deserve — especially in areas where school music programs are limited or nonexistent.

TYMN's goal is to give young musicians across Texas genuine experiences to compete, perform together, and connect with a community of other musicians.
