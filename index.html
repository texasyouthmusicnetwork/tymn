About TYMN

Built to close the gap in music education access.

♩ Insert photo here Student musicians in rehearsal or masterclass — portrait orientation

Student musicians, Austin TX — 2024

Many students only discover high-level opportunities, audition pathways, and strong teachers through personal connections. TYMN exists to change that.

We're a student-led organization that connects young musicians across Texas with the mentorship, guidance, and community that too often depends on who you happen to know — or where you happen to live.

TYMN is currently in its founding phase, launching its first pilot programs in collaboration with partner schools across Texas. We're building from the ground up: transparently, sustainably, and student-first.

Mentorship Pairing experienced student musicians with younger peers
Education Workshops, masterclasses, and audition guidance
Opportunity Access to programs, competitions, and instructors
Community Connecting musicians across schools and cities in Texas
What We Do

Programs & Initiatives

Our programs are designed to give every student musician access to the guidance and resources that make a real difference.

01 Active

Student Mentorship Network

One-on-one guidance from experienced student musicians, tailored to each mentee's goals and instrument.

  • Audition preparation and strategy
  • Practice methods and consistency
  • Performance confidence and stage presence
  • Music theory and fundamentals
02 Active

Workshops & Masterclasses

Group sessions led by student leaders and guest musicians on the topics that matter most for competitive and academic music.

  • Region and All-State audition prep
  • Sight-reading strategies
  • Effective practice methods
  • Performance skills and technique
03 Active

Music Opportunity Guidance

Resources and sessions that help students navigate the landscape of music programs, auditions, and ensembles in Texas and beyond.

  • Summer music program research
  • Regional and state audition pathways
  • Ensemble placement guidance
  • Finding qualified instructors
04 Coming Soon

Community Performances

A future program giving students meaningful opportunities to perform, record, and share their music with the broader community.

  • Recitals and student showcases
  • Community outreach concerts
  • Portfolio recordings for students

Frequently Asked

Common questions about TYMN.

Is TYMN a school club?

No. TYMN is an independent student-led organization that operates across multiple schools and cities in Texas. We partner with schools but are not affiliated with or administered by any single school or district.

Is there a cost to participate?

No. All TYMN programs are free for student participants. Accessibility is central to what we do — we exist specifically to make high-quality music mentorship and guidance available to all Texas students.

What instruments or areas does TYMN support?

TYMN currently supports orchestral and band instruments, with a focus on classical and wind music. As we grow, we aim to expand to more genres and disciplines. Fill out the interest form to let us know your instrument and goals.

How do I join or volunteer?

Students and volunteers can fill out the interest form on this page. We'll be in touch as programs open in your area. You can also join our Discord community to connect with TYMN members and stay updated.

When will programs start?

TYMN is currently in its founding and pilot phase. We're actively building school partnerships and preparing our first workshops. Sign up through the interest form to be notified when programs are available in your area.

How can my school partner with TYMN?

Music educators and administrators can reach out directly via email to discuss bringing TYMN workshops and mentorship programs to your students. We welcome inquiries from schools and music programs across Texas.
