Our Mission

There's more to music than what's on the sheet.

Texas Youth Music Network was founded to solve a recurring problem in music education: for a lot of school music programs, playing an instrument means performing whatever the teacher assigns — with no competitions, no awards, no ensemble playing, and no real chance to connect with other musicians.

TYMN gives young musicians across Texas real chances to compete, perform together, and connect with a community of other musicians — not just play what's already on the stand.
