Common questions about TYMN.

Is TYMN a school club?

No. TYMN is an independent student-led organization that operates across multiple schools and cities in Texas. We partner with schools but are not affiliated with or administered by any single school or district.

Is there a cost to participate?

No. All TYMN programs are free for student participants. Accessibility is central to what we do — we exist specifically to make high-quality music mentorship and guidance available to all Texas students.

What instruments or areas does TYMN support?

TYMN currently supports orchestral and band instruments, with a focus on classical and wind music. As we grow, we aim to expand to more genres and disciplines. Fill out the interest form to let us know your instrument and goals.

How do I join or volunteer?

Students and volunteers can fill out the interest form on this page. We'll be in touch as programs open in your area. You can also join our Discord community to connect with TYMN members and stay updated.

When will programs start?

TYMN is currently in its founding and pilot phase. We're actively building school partnerships and preparing our first workshops. Sign up through the interest form to be notified when programs are available in your area.

How can my school partner with TYMN?

Music educators and administrators can reach out directly via email to discuss bringing TYMN workshops and mentorship programs to your students. We welcome inquiries from schools and music programs across Texas.
