About TYMN

Our Story

Who are we?

For a lot of students, playing an instrument is like a chore— with limited opportunities to showcase their talents and no real way to connect with other musicians outside their own school. TYMN exists to change that.

We're a student-led organization that connects young musicians across Texas with the mentorship, guidance, and community to develop their passion.

TYMN is launching our event—an engaging, virtual competition—in schools all across Texas.

Co-Founder & Executive

Clint Lee

Clint Lee is a junior at LASA and a violinist and pianist from Austin, with over 10 years on both instruments. He has served as concertmaster of the LASA Honors Orchestra and the 2025 TMEA Region 18 Symphony Orchestra, and is a two-time TMEA All-State musician, including the 2025 All-State Philharmonic Orchestra.

As a soloist and concertmaster he has performed at Carnegie Hall in New York and Walt Disney Concert Hall in Los Angeles, and has won prizes at the New York Artists International Competition and the International Golden Classical Music Awards. In 2025 he attended the Boston University Tanglewood Institute. He co-founded TYMN to help expand opportunities for young musicians across Texas.

Co-Founder & Technical Director

Shaoyu Wang

Shaoyu Wang is a junior at LASA who has been playing classical piano for over 10 years, and has won a few of ADMTA competition awards along the way. He also accompanies youth orchestras and soloists, and is a volunteer performer at nursing homes and community events.

Outside of music, Shaoyu loves computer science, and particiaptes in competitive programming, hackathons, and cybersecurity competitions. As TYMN's technical director, he builds and maintains the website and keeps the rest of the organization's tech running. He's excited to help TYMN grow!
